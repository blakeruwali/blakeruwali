The 7pm missed‑call fix

Your next job calls at 7pm. Nobody answers, so they call the next company. This is the order I'd set up an AI phone agent for the calls your office can't pick up, with a person checking. Tick each box before moving on.

01Measure the leak

This week · 30 minutes

  • Pull last week's call log from your phone system.
  • Count unanswered calls by hour. Circle after 5pm, weekends and lunch.
  • For each one: did anyone call back, and how fast?
  • Mark the ones that never got a callback. That's your real number.

02Set the rules in writing

MayGreet, ask what's wrong, take name, address and callback number, ask a few triage questions, text a confirmation.

NeverDiagnose, quote prices, promise arrival times it can't see, talk warranty or rebates.

Hand offGas smell, CO alarm, smoke, flooding, an angry caller, anyone who asks for a person.

03Write the script

  • Greeting under 10 seconds. Say it's an automated assistant.
  • Intake questions: name, callback number, address, what's happening, is anyone without heat or water.
  • Safety calls: your own words for what the caller should do now, then alert a person.
  • Close by reading back the number and what happens next.

"Thanks for calling [Company]. I'm the automated assistant and I can get your request to our team right now. What's going on?"

04Connect it to where jobs live

  • Every call creates a lead or job in your field service software with the transcript.
  • The person on call gets a text and an email for every lead.
  • Can it read your real schedule? Then offer real slots. If not, it promises a callback, never a time.
  • The caller gets a confirmation text about their own request.

05Test it with 20 bad calls

  • Mumbled address · talking over it · wrong number · spam.
  • Existing customer asking about a job already booked.
  • An emergency call: did a person get alerted?
  • Write down every fumble, fix the script, call again.

06Go live narrow

  • Turn it on for after-hours and overflow only.
  • For 2 weeks, a person reviews every recording each morning.
  • Only then add busy-line and lunch-hour overflow.
  • Never route every call through it on day one.

07Review it every week

  • Listen to 10 recordings. Fix one line of the script.
  • Check that every lead got a callback from a person, and how fast.
  • Re-count missed calls against your week-one number. Count, don't guess.
