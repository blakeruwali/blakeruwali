How old is your AC, furnace or water heater? One photo tells you.

Every unit has a data plate with its model and serial number. AI can read it and tell you what you've got.

Copy-paste prompt
Here's a photo of the data plate on my [AC / furnace / water heater]. Read the brand, model and serial number. Tell me: 1) when it was made and how old it is, 2) what refrigerant or fuel it uses, 3) what I should ask before paying for a repair. If you can't read something, say so. Don't guess.

Works in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.

How to do it

  1. Find the sticker

    The metal or paper label with the model and serial number. See where below.

  2. Snap it

    Take a clear, close photo. Make sure the numbers are sharp.

  3. Paste it

    Upload the photo to your AI app, paste the prompt and send.

Where the sticker is

  • AC: on the side panel of the outdoor unit.
  • Furnace: inside the front door.
  • Water heater: on the tank, near the bottom.
