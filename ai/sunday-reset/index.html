Day 2 · Sunday AI Reset

The Sunday AI Reset: 5 prompts, 30 minutes

Plan your whole week in one chat. Works in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Run the prompts in order and save this page for next Sunday.

Never paste passwords, bank or card numbers, or anyone else's private details. Swap names for initials if a message is sensitive.

Start herePaste once

Set up your planning partner

Open one new chat and paste this first. Then run prompts 1 to 5 in the same chat, in order, so prompt 5 can see everything from 1 to 4.

Copy-paste prompt
For the next 30 minutes you're my weekly planning partner. I run [what you do: a business / a job / school]. Be direct and practical. Short answers, plain English, no motivational fluff. If something I share doesn't add up, say so. Wait for my first list.
Prompt 18 min

Empty your head

Copy-paste prompt
Here's everything on my mind for this week, in no order:
[paste a messy list: tasks, worries, ideas, errands]

Sort it into four groups: Must do this week / Should do / Can wait / Hand off or drop.
Then give me my top 3 for the week, and one sentence on why each one matters.
Be ruthless. If something looks like busywork, tell me.

Why it works: your head stops being the to-do list. A messy list in, a clear week out.

Prompt 26 min

Fix your calendar

Copy-paste prompt
Here's my calendar for this week:
[paste it, or type: Mon 9–10 team call, Tue 2–4 client visit, ...]

My top 3 from before are on this chat. Where am I overbooked? What can I move, shorten or cancel?
Find me two 90-minute blocks for focused work on my top 3, and tell me which day is my riskiest.

Why it works: you protect the time before someone else takes it.

Prompt 36 min

Clear what you owe people

Copy-paste prompt
Here are the messages I still owe a reply (I've removed private details):
[paste each one, or a one-line summary of each]

Which ones need me this week, and which can wait or be a one-liner?
Draft a reply of two lines or less for each one that needs me. Match this tone: [friendly / direct / formal].

Why it works: the guilt pile goes to zero, and AI does the typing while you decide what's sent.

Prompt 45 min

Plan your body, not just your work

Copy-paste prompt
Plan my workouts and meals for this week.
I have [X] minutes a day to move, I cook [X] nights, and I [any limits: no gym / vegetarian / early mornings].
Keep it simple and repeatable. Give me a day-by-day plan and one grocery list grouped by store aisle.

Why it works: energy is the thing every plan runs on.

Prompt 55 min

Ask the honest question

Copy-paste prompt
Based on everything I shared today: what am I avoiding?
If I only did one thing this week, what would make it a win?
Give me the first 15-minute step for that one thing, something I can start Monday morning.

Why it works: this is the one that changes my week, and the answer is usually the whole week.

Bonus prompts from my other videos

BonusFrom “Number three”

Let AI draft the work you keep redoing

Copy-paste prompt
I keep writing the same kinds of messages: [emails / quotes / follow-ups].
Here are 3 examples I sent before: [paste, private details removed].
Turn them into a reusable template with blanks in [brackets], in my tone.
Then write today's version for: [the situation].

Rule: AI drafts, you decide. Read every word before you send.

BonusStep 2 of “If I lost everything tomorrow”

Talk to 10 people first

Copy-paste prompt
I want to solve this problem: [the problem you've been annoyed by for years].
Write 8 short questions I can ask 10 people who have this problem.
No leading questions and no pitching. Focus on what they do today, what it costs them, and what they've already tried.
BonusStep 5 of “If I lost everything tomorrow”

Automate what you’ve already done 100 times

Copy-paste prompt
Here's a task I do by hand every week: [describe the steps].
Break it into steps, mark which ones AI or a simple tool could do, and which need me.
Suggest the simplest setup to automate the first part, with no coding if possible.
