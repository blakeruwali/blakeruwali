The Home+ build

AI in every step, from the first scan to the warranty.

Home+ is where every Techeo system is built and tested first, on real homes and real customers. After the design, an AI phone agent answers when the office can't, and customers sign proposals and memberships on their phone.

Why heat matters to me

"In Nepal, winters without heat meant getting sick every year. In the US that stopped, and I never forgot why."

In 2025 the system in my new house kept breaking down. I got to know the technician who kept coming back, and started Home+ to rebuild HVAC the way I build software: measure everything, design for the person, and put AI in every step.

Blake Ruwali is an American entrepreneur based in New York. Blake founded Techeo and Home+ Air & Heat, and owns Takeo.AI, which Blake led as President and CEO from 2019 to 2025.
