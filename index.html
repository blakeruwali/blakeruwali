About

Blake Ruwali is an American entrepreneur based in New York. Blake is the President and CEO of Takeo.AI, a technology education, career services and consulting company, and the founder of Techeo, a firm that builds AI and software products.

Blake started both companies in 2019. In 2025 Blake founded Home+ Air and Heat, a heating and cooling company in Jericho, New York serving Nassau County, Suffolk County and New York City, and runs it as Managing Principal along with Com+ Mechanical and Long Island HVAC Repair.

Blake holds a Master of Science in Information Technology from the University of the Cumberlands and a Bachelor of Business Administration in Global Information Systems from Troy University.
